About Berly

Berly helps make AI compliance work reviewable.

Berly is a desktop AI compliance documentation tool for consultants, lawyers, compliance teams, privacy and risk teams, and AI product teams that need to turn an identified AI system into structured documentation, supporting evidence, review notes, audit history, and exportable documentation packages.

What Berly is

Berly is built for the documentation layer of AI compliance work. It helps teams organize what an AI system does, what data it uses, where human review happens, what evidence supports the record, and what needs to be exported for review.

Berly is not an AI inventory scanner, production monitoring platform, or legal decision system. It sits between AI inventory and professional review.

AI inventory / monitoring
->
Berly documentation + evidence package
->
consultant, lawyer, or compliance review

What Berly does

Structured AI system records

Berly helps organize system purpose, users, workflow context, data notes, human review points, and supporting materials.

Evidence attachment and claim review support

Berly helps users link supporting evidence to documentation claims and flag items for human review.

AI-assisted drafting with human approval

Berly can suggest draft language from project context, but users approve, edit, or reject suggestions before anything is saved.

Risk-related documentation support

Berly helps organize risk-related documentation, impact assessment materials, risk register notes, and review notes for human, legal, or compliance review.

Review notes and audit history

Berly keeps review notes and change history so the documentation process is easier to explain later.

Exportable documentation packages

Berly exports structured PDF packages for human, legal, or compliance review.

What Berly does not do

  • Berly does not provide legal advice.
  • Berly does not determine legal applicability or compliance.
  • Berly does not replace lawyers, auditors, consultants, or compliance professionals.
  • Berly does not discover every AI tool running inside a company.
  • Berly does not monitor production AI systems over time.
  • Berly does not provide final legal or compliance risk assessments or certify AI systems.

Berly is designed to support professional judgment, not replace it.

Product principles

Human-in-the-loop

AI-assisted drafting and evidence review should remain subject to human approval and professional judgment.

Documentation-first

Berly focuses on records, evidence, review notes, and exports rather than broad governance claims.

Local-first desktop workflow

Berly is a downloadable desktop app with no user account required for the product workflow.

Built for review

The goal is to make AI compliance work easier to inspect, explain, and hand to the right reviewer.

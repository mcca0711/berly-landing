The Problem

AI system proof breaks when records and evidence are scattered

Teams may know what an AI system does, but customer, legal, privacy, procurement, audit, and regulatory review gets harder when the system description, data notes, human review points, risks, evidence, and change history live in different places.

The Solution

A local workspace for AI system dossiers

Berly gives users local workspaces for each AI system dossier, including system records, evidence, review notes, change history, and exportable PDF packages. Optional AI assist can help draft or verify parts of the record, but users stay in control of what is saved.

Who Berly is for

Berly supports two related workflows: consultants preparing documentation across client work, and small teams organizing records for their own AI systems before review.

For consultants

Berly helps consultants turn an AI-system assessment into a client-ready dossier. Instead of rebuilding the structure each time in Word or spreadsheets, consultants get a repeatable way to organize the system record, connect it to relevant obligations, attach evidence, and export the result.

For small teams

Berly helps small teams organize their own AI system documentation when they are not ready for a full governance platform. It is useful before legal review, procurement, internal governance review, customer due diligence, audit questions, or any moment where someone asks for proof of how the system was reviewed.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a subscription?

No. Berly is a one-time purchase. Pay once, own the software. No monthly fees, no renewals, no recurring charges.

What is an AI system dossier?

An AI system dossier is the set of records that helps explain an AI system: what it does, what data it uses, where human review happens, what privacy facts need to be captured, what evidence supports the record, and what package can be reviewed later. Berly helps organize those records into a structured workflow.

Does Berly provide legal advice or complete risk assessments?

Berly organizes AI system records, evidence, review notes, and documentation packages. It does not provide legal advice, determine applicability, or certify compliance.

What frameworks are included?

Berly Pro includes selected framework support for the EU AI Act (Regulation 2024/1689), Colorado Automated Decision-Making Technology Act / SB 26-189, Illinois AIVIA and HB 3773, NYC Local Law 144, and California CPRA ADMT. Colorado SB 26-189 repealed and re-enacted Colorado's prior AI provisions; rules are pending. Frameworks help structure the record, but the core job is keeping AI system proof organized. Fields are cross-referenced to NIST AI RMF and ISO/IEC 42001.

Does my client data stay private?

Berly is local-first so sensitive client or internal governance records do not need to be uploaded into another SaaS platform. There is no Berly cloud workspace or user account. When optional AI-assisted features are used, those requests are sent to the supported AI provider connected by the user.

Can I use Berly without an API key?

Yes. Berly can be used manually without an API key. You can fill out the structured fields using the app's guidance, hints, and helper text. AI-assisted drafting and evidence verification require your own supported AI provider API key. Those AI-assisted requests are sent to that provider.

How is this different from a Word template?

A Word template gives you blank fields. Berly provides structured dossier fields, plain-English guidance, AI-assisted drafting with human approval, evidence attachment and review support, selected framework organization, and PDF export with audit trail. You provide the facts and professional judgment. Berly helps organize the dossier package.

What if I need a Mac version?

Berly is currently Windows only (Windows 10/11 64-bit). A Mac version is planned but has no confirmed release date. If Mac support is critical for you, register your interest before purchasing.

What happens when new frameworks are added?

Regulations and guidance can change. Berly may receive maintenance updates for corrections or material changes to included content when identified. Newly added frameworks may be released as paid upgrades. Existing customers receive a discount on future versions.

The Problem

AI compliance documentation gets scattered fast

AI system records often get scattered across policy documents, spreadsheets, vendor notes, emails, model records, and internal reviews. When teams need to explain what an AI system does, what data it uses, where human review happens, and what evidence supports the record, the documentation can become fragmented and difficult to prepare.

The Solution

One workspace for AI system records, evidence, and review notes

Frequently asked questions

Is this a subscription?

No. Berly is a one-time purchase. Pay once, own the software. No monthly fees, no renewals, no recurring charges.

What is AI compliance documentation?

AI compliance documentation is the set of records that helps explain an AI system: what it does, what data it uses, where human review happens, what evidence supports the record, and what documentation package can be reviewed later. Berly helps organize those records into a structured workflow.

Does Berly provide legal advice or complete risk assessments?

No. Berly is not legal advice. It helps organize AI system records, evidence, review notes, risk-related notes, and documentation packages so they can be reviewed by the appropriate human, legal, or compliance professionals.

What frameworks are included?

Berly Pro includes structured documentation support for the EU AI Act (Regulation 2024/1689), Colorado SB 24-205, Illinois AIVIA and HB 3773, NYC Local Law 144, and California CPRA ADMT. All fields are cross-referenced to NIST AI RMF and ISO/IEC 42001.

Does my client data stay private?

Yes. Berly is a desktop application. Your documentation stays on your local machine. There is no cloud backend and no user account. If you choose to use AI-assisted features, requests are sent only through your own API key to the AI provider you selected.

How is this different from a Word template?

A Word template gives you blank fields. Berly provides structured documentation fields, plain-English guidance, AI-assisted drafting with human approval, evidence attachment and review support, cross-framework organization, and PDF export with audit trail. You provide the facts and professional judgment. Berly helps organize the documentation package.

What if I need a Mac version?

Berly is currently Windows only (Windows 10/11 64-bit). A Mac version is on our roadmap but has no confirmed release date. If Mac support is critical for you, contact support@berly.app before purchasing.

What happens when new frameworks are added?

New frameworks will be released as separate paid upgrades. Existing customers receive a discount on future versions. Your current purchase is not a subscription and does not include future framework additions automatically, but you will not pay full price as a returning customer.
